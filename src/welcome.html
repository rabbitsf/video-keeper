Welcome to Video Keeper

Save videos from the pages you visit as ordinary MP4 files in your Downloads folder.

1 Pin the button

Click the puzzle-piece icon in your browser's toolbar, then the pin next to Video Keeper, so the button is always one click away.

2 Download a video

Open a page with a video and click the Video Keeper button. It lists the videos on that page — click Download. For streaming videos you can pick the quality and watch the progress; you can close the popup while it works.

If nothing shows up, start playing the video and open the popup again. Copy-protected services (Netflix, Disney+, Prime Video and similar) and live streams can't be downloaded.

! Allow access to websites

Video Keeper needs permission to see the videos on the pages you visit.

3 YouTube downloads optional

YouTube needs the free Video Keeper Helper, a small Mac app that does the download. You install it once; after that it keeps itself up to date.

Checking for Video Keeper Helper…

After installing, quit and reopen your browser, then click Check again.

Only download videos you have the right to: your own, openly licensed ones, or where the website allows it. Video Keeper doesn't collect any data.
